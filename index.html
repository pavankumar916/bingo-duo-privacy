BINGO DUO

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Bingo Duo browser extension handles information when people play two-player Bingo games using room codes.

Information the extension handles

To run a multiplayer game, Bingo Duo handles:

  • Display name: the name a player enters in the extension. Players should use a nickname and avoid entering sensitive personal information.
  • Anonymous Firebase identifier: Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous user identifier for a browser session/account. Bingo Duo does not ask players to provide an email address or password.
  • Game and room data: room code, player slots, Bingo boards, marked numbers, whose turn it is, the last move, game result, round number, and rematch requests.

How information is used

This information is used only to create and join rooms, associate a player with their game session, synchronize gameplay between the two players, display turns and results, and support rematches.

Cloud service provider and sharing

Bingo Duo uses Google Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database to authenticate players anonymously and store/synchronize game data. Data sent to Firebase is processed by Google as the service provider under its applicable terms and privacy practices.

Game room data is available to participants in that room so the multiplayer game can work. Do not treat a room code as a place to store private information. Bingo Duo is not designed to sell player data or use it for advertising. The game does not need browsing history or website content to function.

Information not intentionally collected by Bingo Duo

Bingo Duo does not ask players for passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, or precise location. Browsing history and website content are not needed for gameplay. The extension should not be used to submit sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

Room and game data may remain in Firebase until it is deleted by the publisher. The current extension does not provide an in-game option to delete a room or its stored data. To ask about deletion, use the support contact provided on the Bingo Duo Microsoft Edge Add-ons listing.

Children and sensitive information

Bingo Duo is a casual game, not a service for submitting personal information. Players should use nicknames and must not enter passwords, contact details, or other sensitive information as their display name.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the extension's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a new version is published.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning room data, use the support contact provided on the Bingo Duo Microsoft Edge Add-ons listing.
